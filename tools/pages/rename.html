Check your name before the card

A guided test you run on your own phone. Type the name into the in-game box, tell the lab what you see, and it finds the exact character that causes trouble, so the card only goes on a name you have already seen work.

Does testing use the card?Typing into the name box and looking at it should not, because the card is spent when you press the final confirm button. Do not press confirm during the tests. Some checks, such as a name already taken or a word filter, may only run on confirm, so a good preview is encouraging, not a guarantee.
