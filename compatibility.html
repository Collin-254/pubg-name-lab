Compatibility · Evidence, not guesses

What is known, and how well

A character being valid Unicode does not mean PUBG MOBILE accepts it. This page keeps four levels of evidence apart, shows what is official and what nobody has published, and lets you record your own in-game tests.

Nothing on this site is marked verified yet. Results you record below are shown as “You” and are never presented as fact.

02 · Official, and not published

What PUBG MOBILE has said

Each item links to its source and shows when it was last checked. Rules can change.

03 · Test lab

Test it in your own game

Pick a pack, type each probe name into the in-game rename field, and tell the lab what happened. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to submit.

04 · Your results

Test log

Stored in this browser only. Export it, or send it to the project as a GitHub issue so a maintainer can add the results as community-reported records.

05 · Published records

Community-reported and verified

Only entries with a game version, platform and date appear here. Verified means reproduced with a screenshot or recording.

How a record is added: a report arrives as a GitHub issue, the maintainer checks the evidence, then pastes it into PNL.COMPAT.records in js/compat.js. Reports stay “community-reported” until reproduced.
